"Sum up 20 November's world news in one image" ($250, top 50 paid, free entry).

## What the image shows

A dense newspaper front page for **Friday 20 November 2026 — 45 stories
across 6 continents**, with a world map carrying 12 numbered story markers:

- **Anthropic's ANT soars 34% on debut** — shares open at $111.50, briefly
  pushing the Claude maker past a $2 trillion valuation.
- **Myanmar guilty of genocide** — the ICJ rules 9–6 that Myanmar committed
  genocide against the Rohingya, the first time the world court has found a
  state guilty; reparations ordered.
- **Cyclone Akvan hits India** — landfall near Gopalpur, Odisha, with
  300,000+ people evacuated and 47 trains cancelled.
- **Kyiv: embassies hit in raid** — Russian drones kill one person and
  damage the Austrian and Dutch embassies and St Volodymyr's Cathedral.

Plus a six-continent region column, 26 briefs (COP31's final day, the
US–Venezuela military pact, Seoul's Han Duck-soo retrial, Fiji's drought
disaster, the NZ–India Test, GTA VI's rollout) and a footer ticker.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
# writes entry.png to ./out (or $MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR)
```

Python 3.13 standard library only. No network. The program copies the
finished `entry.png` (1024×1024, under 1 MiB), so both runs write
byte-identical output.
